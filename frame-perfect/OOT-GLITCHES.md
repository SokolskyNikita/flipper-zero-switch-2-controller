# Ocarina of Time (Switch 2) glitches

A list of the glitches known in the 2026 Ocarina of Time remake as of 16 November 2026. The [last section](#being-considered-for-frame-perfect) says which of them are being considered for Frame Perfect.

- **Sources:** the Ocarina of Time 2026 speedrunning Discord (glitch-hunting channel, the routing forum and the per-glitch video threads), the ZSR wiki, StratDB, YouTube videos and run commentary. Links are in [Sources](#sources).
- **Dates** are UTC and are a best guess: usually the day the first clip or thread was posted.
- **Credits** use the names people post under. Where a thread or video names a finder, that is what is listed.
- **"Works on"** comes from version tags and reports in those threads. "Not stated" means nobody said.
- **Any% impact** is for runs from a fresh file (no NG+), judged against the route used in the 2:05:25 run of 15 November.
- **Leaderboards:** Frame Perfect is for research, practice and demonstrations. Leaderboard rules were still being written when this was compiled; assume scripted inputs are not allowed in submitted runs.

## Route and records (16 Nov)

- **Fastest run found:** [2:05:25 by Rick Schmidty](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-Wxm7craXQA), 15 November. The time includes a 24-second allowance, because cartridge loads are about 24 seconds slower than digital over a run and the community is still deciding how to treat that.
- **Route outline:** Deku Tree with cutscene skips, Zelda, bombs from Dodongo's Cavern, Din's Fire, Shadow Temple as a child (which makes Link adult), Hookshot, Megaton Hammer, then Spirit and Forest for the bow, Ganon's Castle trials and the tower collapse.
- **No longer needed:** Jabu-Jabu, finishing Dodongo's Cavern, the Song of Time, the Ice Cavern and Iron Boots, the Water Temple and Longshot, and finishing the Forest and Fire temples, which are entered only for the bow and hammer.
- **Why Spirit comes before Forest:** the favoured Iron Boots skip setup needs a shield for its turns.
- **Still required:** the trials, a bottle (only for blue fire in the trials), and the Light Arrow cutscene, which also hands over the Master Sword.

## High impact

### Jumpslash cancel

- **Also known as:** JSC, IJSC (infinite jumpslash cancel), IJS, infinite jump, infinite height glitch, super jump, sword jump, Deku Stick jump, hammer hover, hovering.
- **What it does:** each jump slash gives upward speed. Putting the weapon away and re-drawing it in mid-air allows another jump slash before Link lands, so the chain climbs for as long as it is kept up.
- **How:** target and jump slash with a Deku Stick or the Megaton Hammer, open the item wheel, pick another item, let the game run until the weapon leaves Link's hand, pick the weapon again, close the wheel and press the weapon button, then reopen the wheel. Repeat.
- **Height cap:** a normal jump stores the height it was made from, and jump slashes stop lifting Link above it. Sidehops, backflips and jump slashes do not set it. Returning to the title screen clears it, and the ZSR wiki says changing age at the Master Sword does too. The cap is an absolute height that carries between maps, and maps sit at different heights: a jump in Hyrule Field sets a cap high enough to hover to the sky in Gerudo Fortress. Runners plan where they last jumped so the stored height is enough for the next hover.
- **Variants:**
  - *Backwards jumpslash* (bloonstd6master, 10 Nov): with no sword equipped, target, hold back, press A and tap ZL twice. The ZSR wiki starts it from a backhop and releases the target button once the chain is going. Link hovers while moving backwards, which gains a lot of height in a small space, and holding target again turns it back into a forward hover three cancels later. The first Forest Escape setup used it.
  - *Faster start* (Kenny-Dumminik, 11 Nov): pressing the weapon button and A together while holding forward starts the hover without a first swing. Reported as possibly frame perfect.
  - *Pause method* (flutterdashie64, 8 Nov): the same chain driven by the pause button.
  - *Higher hover* (Tholive_ and Ataappi): a jump slash lets a Hover Boots hover start above the platform.
- **Found by:** Ataappi, 7 Nov. The mechanics were written up by nathanisbored.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** high. It gets child Link into the Shadow Temple, replaces most dungeon climbing, and is part of nearly every other skip here.
- **Video:** [Jumpslash Cancel Glitch Tutorial by gymnast86](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4HSZ6GMQCZk).

### Actor jump

- **Also known as:** actor bouncing, ocarina jump, bean jump, bean flower jump, chest jump, child super jump, hookshot jump.
- **What it does:** jumping on the frame an aim or ocarina animation ends, while standing on a movable or interactive object, launches Link far higher than a normal jump. A moving object adds its speed.
- **How:** stand on an actor (chest, gravestone, box, block, bean plant), hold target, take out the ocarina, turn the camera all the way down, put the ocarina away and press jump a moment later. The hookshot, bow and slingshot work in place of the ocarina. Child Link can do it with only the Hylian Shield, and RoboRotembo showed on 9 Nov that opening the item wheel works with no item at all.
- **Variants:**
  - *Bean jump* (Vidya James, 6 Nov): the first form found, off a moving bean plant.
  - *Directional actor jump* (Hellios, 10 Nov): releasing the ocarina quickly steers the launch.
  - *Hitstop actor jump* (RoboRotembo, 16 Nov): see "Hitstop launches" below.
- **Found by:** Vidya James (bean jump), then Ataappi showed it works on any actor and with the shield alone.
- **Works on:** 1.1.1 by many reports. On 1.0 it appears in setups alongside 1.0-only tricks.
- **Any% impact:** high. It was the first way into the Shadow Temple early and to the Mirror Shield and Silver Gauntlets. Much of that is now done with jumpslash cancels, but routes still use it where it is quicker, such as the Gerudo Fortress skip.
- **Video:** [Actor Jumping by Kethaldo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Rop0oeZVk24). A fixed setup: [Bean Jump for Shadow Temple Early by RoboRotembo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1EIvnoPTQ48).

### Puberty skip

- **Also known as:** child skip, early adult, "the Door of Time skip".
- **What it does:** finishing an adult dungeon as child Link plays the sage cutscene and returns Link to the overworld as an adult, with the Kokiri Sword still equipped. The Door of Time never has to open. The Master Sword then arrives through the Light Arrow cutscene, which checks only for the Shadow and Spirit medallions.
- **Why it works:** the Shadow Temple's blue warp always loads the adult Graveyard map, and a map forces Link's age to match it. Current age and "has done the Master Sword sequence" are tracked separately, so Link is adult without that sequence ever running.
- **Found by:** oussama and tvbrayden, per the ZSR wiki. It was being passed around on 6 Nov and tvbrayden wrote it up on 7 Nov. Press coverage credits ZFG with reporting it.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** high. With the Song of Time skip it removes two of the three child dungeons.
- **Video:** no good dedicated one; see the [2:05:25 run](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-Wxm7craXQA).

### Save/load respawn manipulation

- **Also known as:** save warp, save/load clip, out of bounds to in bounds, loading a file in mid-air.
- **What it does:** a save stores Link's last safe position, and a load runs a second, stricter check. If Link saves somewhere the game does not accept, the load places him at a nearby valid spot, which can be on the other side of a wall. Playing the ocarina out of bounds pins the save position there.
- **Found by:** wxyz (9 Nov, out of bounds back in bounds), GlitchesAndStuff (9 Nov, loading in mid-air), Poodleskirt (10 Nov, loading into a place never visited), Dusty_Carpet (13 Nov, ocarina pins the position).
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** high. The Song of Time skip and the Iron Boots skip are both built on it.
- **Video:** [Ocarina saving our position from out of bounds by Dusty_Carpet](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fRGNLxWWfDc).

### Bomb T-pose

- **Also known as:** bomb pose (the ZSR wiki's name), bomb/chu T-pose, bomb clip.
- **What it does:** putting the ocarina away while Link is breathing in leaves his upper-body animation in a wider state. Pulling a bomb or bombchu while moving then swings his arms out and pushes him through nearby collision.
- **How:** hold any note until it wobbles (3 seconds or more), release it, wait half a second, put the ocarina away, then pull a bomb while moving. Nova found that any of Link's inhales works if the put-away is timed to it. Jumping, climbing, rolling, sliding, throwing or picking up a bomb, pulling one while standing still, or taking damage ends the state.
- **Found by:** Pasty (PastyPixel), 12 Nov.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** high, as the clip used in the Song of Time skip.
- **Video:** [Any% Is Now 20 Minutes Faster by ThreeLetterName](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4V1lRs6J-Sk), which shows the T-pose and the skip.

### Song of Time skip

- **Also known as:** SoT skip, SoT block skip.
- **What it does:** gets past the Song of Time block in the Forest Temple without the song. Hover to the top of a pillar, save, do the bomb T-pose to clip into the corner, save again and reload: Link loads into the courtyard beyond the door.
- **Found by:** Ultraspeed7 found the save/load spot (11 Nov), Pasty the T-pose, and ontrigger posted the first full clip on 12 Nov. Later setups by Lid, Layz, jakezsr and CrypticJacknife cut the hover from about 60 cancels to under 20.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** high. The song was needed only for this block, so Dodongo's Cavern and Jabu-Jabu no longer have to be finished. About 20 minutes.
- **Video:** [Forest Temple SoT skip by ontrigger](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wEKZvrajzQE). Tutorial: [ThreeLetterName](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4V1lRs6J-Sk).

### Iron Boots skip (Invalid Respawn)

- **Also known as:** irons skip, bow room void, "bomb respawn".
- **What it does:** enters the Forest Temple bow room without the Iron Boots. Playing the ocarina in a ceiling corner of the east courtyard sets the respawn point there. Bombs placed on that exact spot make the point invalid, and voiding out (which does not despawn bombs, unlike a reload) moves Link to the next valid place, inside the Stalfos room.
- **Found by:** Ultraspeed7, 14 Nov. Bomb-throw lineup by DrDiabeteus. The setup most runners use, with shield turns and a single bomb throw, is by sva (15 Nov).
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** high. With the Longshot skip it removes the Ice Cavern and Water Temple. About 20 minutes.
- **Video:** [Iron Boots Skip from Actor Jump](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pyZic3Uyp08). Without Hookshot: [Song of Time Skip (No Iron Boots / Hookshot) by karterfreak](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RyWAgiSeS9M).

### Collapse Longshot skip

- **Also known as:** Longshot skip, LS skip. The bombchu version is called the Rizz skip, after the cutscene it skips.
- **What it does:** the tower escape has a gap that Link and Zelda cross with the Longshot. Dying at one checkpoint and walking back despawns a flame wall, which opens a way onto the broken arch. From there the plain Hookshot reaches the target.
- **Two methods:**
  - *Hookshot method:* sidehop onto the arch, slide to a lower ledge with the Hover Boots and shoot the target. A version without Hover Boots exists.
  - *Rizz skip:* hover higher and send a bombchu around the corner so the cutscene never plays. The bombchu's path varies, so it is less reliable.
- **Found by:** zfg mapped the void plane on 10 Nov. HenglesZ found the flame-wall despawn. On 14 Nov dannyb and RoboRotembo got both methods working within about an hour of each other.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** high. The Longshot's last required use is gone, and with it the Water Temple.
- **Video:** [Skip Longshot in Tower Collapse by dannyb](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-VOZJwH2BUc). Rizz skip: [Taechuk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rEj3BjL5gPk).

## Medium impact

### Item-wheel frame advance (pause buffering)

- **What it does:** the game pauses while the item wheel or pause menu is open and runs for a few frames between openings. Inputs can be placed on single frames this way.
- **How:** tap the wheel button repeatedly, or pause with Plus, hold the wheel button and unpause, which gives one frame to act before the wheel appears.
- **Found by:** Caidren, 5 Nov. The pause-menu version is by Poodleskirt (8 Nov). Yatagarasu noted that talking to Navi also pauses the game while attack cooldowns keep running.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** medium. It is how humans hit the frame-tight parts of other glitches, at a cost in time.
- **Video:** none dedicated.

### Hookshot storage

- **Also known as:** HS, hook storage.
- **What it does:** if the hookshot is interrupted on the frame it attaches, the attach point is never cleared. The next hookshot release pulls Link to that stored point, through climbable walls and grates.
- **How:** place two bombs so the first sets off the second and only the second hits Link. Stand against the wall, hold the hookshot, and release on the first frame of the first explosion. Runners pause buffer to find that frame. Unequipping the Iron Boots or falling from a slope can also interrupt it.
- **Uses:** clips behind ladders and grates, the boss key skips below, and fast travel across a map (*fast hookshot storage movement*, Dusty_Carpet). It works on any actor, including ones the hookshot cannot normally target, such as push blocks and redeads.
- **Found by:** NathanLoto, 8 Nov. The two-bomb setup is by GlitchesAndStuff.
- **Works on:** 1.0 only. Releasing with the target unloaded crashes the game.
- **Any% impact:** medium, and only for 1.0 runners.
- **Video:** [Hookshot Storage Setup by Pizzabite](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sgmJNYPChXc).

### Boss key skips

- **What it does:** hookshot storage clips lead out of bounds and into the boss rooms of the Forest, Fire, Spirit and Shadow temples.
- **Found by:**
  - *Forest:* Dusty_Carpet and Ultraspeed7, 11 Nov.
  - *Fire:* NathanLoto, 11 Nov, with a better path by Ultraspeed7.
  - *Spirit:* Dusty_Carpet, 11 Nov; the setup most people use is by minimini352.
  - *Shadow:* Zas, NathanLoto, GlitchesAndStuff and Dusty_Carpet, 12 Nov.
- **Works on:** 1.0 only. A Fire Temple version using the Yiga Warp exists but needs NG+.
- **Any% impact:** medium. The Spirit skip is the one that matters, since Spirit is still completed. It goes straight to Twinrova's room, so it also skips the Nabooru fight.
- **Video:** [Spirit Temple BK skip by minimini352](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irhpbegsgSc). Others: [Forest](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1ZHPVLBWiP0), [Fire](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Uhi7EqcHS4g), [Shadow](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QO6Mk7i-o6I).

## Low impact

### Hover Boots bottle extension

- **Also known as:** infinite Hover Boots (the ZSR wiki's working name), bottle hover.
- **What it does:** swinging an empty bottle during a hover drops Link slightly and resets the hover timer, so the hover continues. It depends on the height of the floor below and fails over voids.
- **Found by:** Quib, 7 Nov.
- **Works on:** not stated.
- **Any% impact:** low. It is one way across the Fire trial.
- **Video:** [Hoverboots Jump Glitch by Kalugero](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=clc_DyfvBCE).

### Hover jump

- **Also known as:** jumping out of Hover Boots.
- **What it does:** near the end of a hover, turning around and back allows a jump from mid-air. Unequipping the boots on the second turn gives a forward jump, and it works in any direction.
- **Found by:** Dry4Haz, 7 Nov.
- **Works on:** not stated.
- **Any% impact:** low. Small skips in the Ice Cavern and trials.
- **Video:** [Ice Cavern Red Ice Hover Jump](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TrLwy1EG1es).

### Deku Tree B1 skip

- **What it does:** reaches the basement ledge directly, skipping most of the dungeon. A jumpslash cancel ledge grab (doublea) made it easier.
- **Found by:** Ataappi, 7 Nov.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** low.
- **Video:** none verified.

### Gerudo Fortress skip

- **What it does:** hover or actor jump onto invisible floors above the fortress and drop into the load zone on the far side. Routing posts call the related trick at the gate the Gerudo gate skip.
- **Found by:** python, 8 Nov. Actor-jump version by Tach, from a proof of concept by Vinek.
- **Works on:** not stated.
- **Any% impact:** low.
- **Video:** [Actor Jump Gerudo Fortress Skip by Tach](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7nm7BUvUANg).

### Cutscene skips

- **What it does:** hovering over trigger zones skips the Mido conversation, the Deku Tree cutscene and the Hyrule Field owl.
- **Found by:** several people between 8 and 13 Nov. dannyb posted the field owl skip.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Side effect:** the day and night cycle only starts once the Hyrule Field owl cutscene has been watched. Skipping it freezes time at early morning for the whole game, which rules the skip out for 100%.
- **Any% impact:** low. Seconds each.
- **Video:** none verified.

### Fast songs

- **Also known as:** hold tech.
- **What it does:** bending a note up or down counts as the neighbouring note, so most songs can be played with fewer button presses by holding one button and bending.
- **Found by:** Poodleskirt, 11 Nov. Cheat sheet by CrypticJacknife.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** low.
- **Video:** [Fast Song Examples by Myth197](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vs0N5NA9Lvo).

### Boss quick kills

- **What it does:** faster boss fights: one- and two-cycle child bosses, Din's Fire to open Bongo Bongo, hammer jump cancels for damage, a two-cycle Twinrova.
- **Found by:** bradyONE (child bosses, 5 Nov), Vidya James (hammer cancel damage), Calebhart42 (two-cycle Bongo Bongo), karterfreak (Twinrova) and others.
- **Works on:** not stated.
- **Any% impact:** low.
- **Video:** [Child Boss Quick-kill Methods by bradyONE](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cdgZDQoIeqw).

### Castle guard shot

- **What it does:** a slingshot hit stuns a Hyrule Castle guard, which allows a shorter line through the grounds.
- **Found by:** not known. StratDB dates it 12 Nov. Runners were still working out how far it goes on 16 Nov.
- **Works on:** not stated.

## None so far

These are real but are not in the fresh-file Any% route, either because they need NG+, are slower than the alternative, or have no use yet.

### Yiga Warp

- **Also known as:** save location transfer, redead warp.
- **What it does:** returning to the title screen from the redead bridge in the tower collapse leaves the escape checkpoint position marked valid. After that, a manual save and reload in any area places Link at those coordinates on the current map. The same spot lands above the Fire Temple boss room and above a Water Temple room, among others.
- **Found by:** Ymuy, 14 Nov. Mechanism by ontrigger.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** none for fresh-file runs, because the escape has to be reached first. It is being treated as an NG+ trick.
- **Video:** [YigaBomb/Load by Brando](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2ZN8M4rvC2Y).

### Forest Escape

- **What it does:** leaves Kokiri Forest before the Deku Tree. Hover up with about 100 jump slashes (the first setup was 55 backwards, a turn, then 29 forwards), cross above the invisible walls, then fall into water and swim-dash out.
- **Found by:** Ataappi, 10 Nov, who credits TurothQueen with the idea for the newer straight-line setup. Earlier out-of-bounds work in the forest is by Ram and Snugggles.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.
- **Any% impact:** none yet. It is easier without a sword, and a swordless route loses more time later than it saves. Nothing after the forest needs the Deku Tree, so it would matter if it got faster.
- **Video:** [Kokiri Forest Escape by karterfreak](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hHll6FaJa1s).

### Temple of Time door skip

- **What it does:** hover to the top of the Temple of Time and drop past the Door of Time.
- **Found by:** Truman, 15 Nov.
- **Works on:** 1.1.1 (tagged). 1.0 not stated.
- **Any% impact:** none. The first Master Sword pull only works if the door was opened by the Song of Time at the altar, so getting past the door achieves nothing.

### Hitstop launches

- **What it does:** landing a hit freezes Link for a few frames. Doing an actor jump during that freeze, for example hitting a Poe with the hammer while standing on a moving gravestone, gives a much larger launch. Earlier unexplained launches off Stalfos and Iron Knuckles look like the same effect.
- **Also known as:** hitstop actor jump, enemy-hit launches.
- **Found by:** the first clip, by RoyArashi, was shared on 5 Nov and Heather! opened a thread on 7 Nov. RoboRotembo tied it to hitstop on 16 Nov.
- **Works on:** not stated.
- **Any% impact:** none yet. It is hours old and not understood.
- **Video:** [Hammer Hitstop Actor Jump by RoboRotembo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QbIiTSNh678).

### Geddan boost

- **What it does:** child Link holding the Biggoron Sword at low health, facing a wall, is launched a long way by a bomb blast. It usually kills him, so it costs one or two fairies. Getting the sword onto child Link takes an age change with it equipped, which is why it has no use in a run.
- **Found by:** CaspianFS, 11 Nov.
- **Works on:** not stated.
- **Video:** [No IJSC shadow entrance by caspian](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IZfIzqN-IjU).

### Ocarina push and dive

- **Also known as:** elevator clip.
- **What it does:** Link can be pushed while playing the ocarina and passes through void planes in that state. Bombs dropped against a corner before opening the ocarina can push him out of bounds.
- **Found by:** panchosteve, 10 Nov. Benjamin Datum confirmed it underwater.
- **Works on:** 1.0 and 1.1.1.

### Cucco teleport

- **What it does:** a cucco that lands in deep water respawns at its last safe spot. Picking it up as it enters the water drags Link part of the way there, including out of bounds. It gets adult Link into the Bottom of the Well.
- **Found by:** Leem, 11 Nov. The well entry is by Benjamin Datum, Joshmesh, Toti Sauce, Piggachew and Leem.
- **Works on:** not stated.
- **Video:** [Enter Bottom of the Well as Adult](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tDpLbEltLy4).

### Platform momentum storage

- **What it does:** the speed of a moving platform is kept after leaving it, as long as Link lands only on certain ground types. Jumpslash cancels and Hover Boots inherit it.
- **Found by:** ontrigger, 11 Nov.
- **Works on:** not stated.
- **Video:** [platform momentum storage by ontrigger](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AnZz7z6EUVE).

## Glossary

- **Actor:** any object the game simulates separately from the level: chests, boxes, gravestones, enemies.
- **BK:** boss key.
- **DoT:** Door of Time. **ToT:** Temple of Time. **SoT:** Song of Time.
- **HS:** hookshot storage.
- **JSC / IJSC:** jumpslash cancel, and a chain of them.
- **LACS:** the Light Arrow cutscene.
- **NG+:** a run that depends on something set up in an earlier playthrough.
- **OoB:** out of bounds.
- **Void plane:** an invisible surface that respawns Link when he touches it.

## Being considered for Frame Perfect

Frame Perfect sends buttons and full-tilt stick directions on exact frames. It has no gyro, no partial stick tilt, and it cannot see the screen, so it cannot line Link up. A glitch is a candidate when the hard part is timing or a long repeated input chain, and the position can be set by hand first.

Already working: the jumpslash cancel as child Link with a Deku Stick ([SUPERJUMP.md](../SUPERJUMP.md), `sequences/oot_superjump.txt`, [demo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hTiaGGzQzuU)).

Candidates, most useful first:

1. **Actor jump.** The jump has to follow the ocarina put-away within a short window, and some people pause buffer it. The camera move is a full-tilt right stick. Launch height varies between attempts for reasons nobody has pinned down, and identical inputs are the way to find out why.
2. **More jumpslash cancel variants.** The hammer version for adult Link, the backwards variant, and the faster start, which is reported as possibly frame perfect. Also a tighter loop for places with a low ceiling or a void plane, where each cancel has to gain as little height as possible.
3. **Long jumpslash cancel chains.** The Forest Escape needs about 100 in a row, and the hard part for a person is not making one mistake. The Song of Time skip needs between about 17 and 60 depending on the setup. Short chains cover the Deku Tree B1 skip and the cutscene skips.
4. **Hookshot storage setup.** The hookshot has to be released on the first explosion frame, which people find by pause buffering. A sequence could place the bombs and release on a counted frame, if the fuse length is constant. People counting by hand report 8 to 12 frames for the last flash, so that needs measuring first. It only helps on version 1.0.
5. **Bomb T-pose.** The long-note method is easy by hand. A sequence would make the quicker version, timed to one of Link's inhales, repeatable.
6. **Hover Boots bottle hover and hover jump.** The hover is a steady rhythm of bottle swings. For the jump, people remap buttons so they can press unequip and jump together; a sequence presses both on the same frame.
7. **Hitstop launches.** The hit and the jump are frame-linked, so a sequence could help work out the timing. The enemy has to be in the right place, which makes it hard to repeat.
8. **Fast songs.** Songs are fixed button strings, so this is simple to add. The gain is small.

Not candidates:

- **Iron Boots skip setup.** Its shield turns and spin cancels are fixed button inputs, but run commentary describes a slow walk into a corner, which needs partial stick tilt.
- **Collapse Longshot skip.** It is movement along narrow collision and a precise aim that runners do with gyro.
- **Boss key skips.** The storage setup and the hovers are covered above. The out-of-bounds navigation between them is not scriptable without seeing the screen.
- **Boss quick kills.** The damage strings are scriptable, but bosses move and some phases are random.
- **Everything else** is routing, menu work or positioning: the puberty skip, save/load respawns, the Yiga Warp and the rest.
